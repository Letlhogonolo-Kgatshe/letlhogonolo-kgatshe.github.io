The problem

A small practice offering online consultations was running a US template site with placeholder reviews and New York contact details. Patients need to see quickly how to book, what can be treated online, and when to call an ambulance instead.

What I built

  • Five pages: home, services, a three-step booking flow, medication orders, and contact with frequently asked questions.
  • A booking flow (service and call type, then day and time, then details) with inline validation, a review screen and an add-to-calendar file.
  • Booking and order requests go to the practice through WhatsApp or email, so no health data is stored on a server.
  • The emergency numbers (10177 and 112) on every page, and a sticky Call, WhatsApp and Book bar on phones.
  • An "open now" badge and opening hours driven by one configuration file.

Key decisions

No server-side health data

The site is static. Requests are sent through WhatsApp or email, so nothing sensitive sits in a database, in line with POPIA.

Honest about online care

Every page shows the emergency numbers, and the services page lists the warning signs that need in-person care.

No invented reviews

The template testimonials were removed. Real, consented patient reviews can be added later.

Results

  • Live on GitHub Pages. The practice's real phone, WhatsApp and address are still to be added, in one configuration file.
  • Redesigned in 2026 with a Quiet, Showcase and Midnight style switcher; the choice is saved in the browser and shared with my other sites.
