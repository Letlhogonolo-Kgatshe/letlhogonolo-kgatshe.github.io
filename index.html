About

I'm fascinated by what data can reveal: the connections hiding in a dataset that nobody thought to look for. I have an eye for detail and a natural pull towards patterns, which helps me take abstract problems and make them concrete.

AI can match patterns at scale, but people are still better at stepping back, seeing the whole landscape and asking the right question. That's where I want to work: using modern tools to distil a problem down to its essence, then letting the data answer it.

I'm especially drawn to finance and consumer behaviour, from spending trends to the habits underneath them. I don't believe in reinventing the wheel. Often the simplest solution is the best one.
