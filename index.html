About

I'm fascinated by what data can reveal: the connections in a dataset that nobody thought to look for. I have an eye for detail and a natural pull towards patterns, which helps me turn abstract problems into concrete ones.

AI can match patterns at scale, but people are still better at stepping back, seeing the whole picture and asking the right question. That's where I want to work: using modern tools to boil a problem down to its essence, then letting the data answer it.

I'm especially drawn to finance and consumer behaviour, from spending trends to the habits behind them. I don't believe in reinventing the wheel; often the simplest solution is the best one.
